## The outcome, and nothing beyond it

The work is done when the user's stated outcome is met and the user can use it; everything else is cost.

- **Nothing is built to prove, record or guard the work unless the user asked for it**: no harness, controller, proposal, receipt, ledger, evidence archive, hash list, extra review, or check beyond the one the spec names.
- **The smallest change that meets the spec.** What the spec does not hold goes to the user with what it costs and what it buys: a spec gap, for this sprint, when the outcome or a requirement needs it; otherwise a scope increase, named as one. Removing a requirement shrinks the plan.
- **Cutting is always on offer**: whoever stops puts "stop here" and "cut to what the outcome needs" beside the fixes, with what the step has cost.
