## The outcome, and nothing beyond it

The work is done when the user's stated outcome is met and usable; everything else is cost.

- **Nothing is built to prove, record or guard the work unless the user asked for it**: no harness, controller, proposal, receipt, ledger, evidence archive, hash list, extra review, or check beyond the spec's.
- **The smallest change that meets the spec.** What the spec lacks goes to the user with its cost: a spec gap, for this sprint, if the outcome or a requirement needs it, else a named scope increase. Removing a requirement shrinks the plan.
- **A small find is fixed now, with a heads-up**, by whoever may change the file, per conventions.md "Small finds".
- **Every offer is priced** (backlog, deferred, out of scope): its effort in time ("about an hour"; S, M, L) and a recommendation; S defaults to fix now; an unpriced one goes back.
- **Proof past the step's size stops.** Proof costing more than the step goes to the user with its cost, "ship as is" on offer.
- **Cutting is always on offer**: whoever stops puts "stop here" and "cut to what the outcome needs" beside the fixes, with the cost so far.
