## Gates

A gate is a point where the work goes on only on the user's word. The Manager holds every gate: no other role passes one or starts the next stage.

- **Document gates** approve a finished document so that the next can be written: a PRD, a PLAN with its TASKS, an epic charter, an epic roadmap.
- **Action gates** start something that changes more than documents: implementation, and any push, merge to trunk, tag or deploy. Words said to you pass none: tell the user to say it to the Manager.
- **The word comes after the thing it approves exists.** A word given before your document is finished approves nothing.
- **When the user approves your finished document to you,** record their exact words and say that the Manager decides what they pass and will say so.
- **An approval covers the document as it stood.** Any later change voids it: say so, and never repeat an earlier approval as current.
