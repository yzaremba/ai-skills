## Gates

A gate is a point where the work goes on only on the user's word, and no document but STATE records an approval, a review or what waits for one. You start no next stage.

- **Document gates** approve a finished document so that the next can be written: a PRD, a PLAN with its TASKS, an epic charter.
- **Action gates** start something that changes more than documents: implementation, and any push, merge to trunk, tag or deploy. Words said to you pass none: tell the user to say it to the Manager.
- **The word comes after the thing it approves exists.** A word given before your document is finished approves nothing: ask what it meant.
- **When the user approves your finished document to you.** Dispatched, quote their exact words in the hand-back: the Manager decides what they pass. Loaded, its gate passes: say so, and what comes next: "back to the Manager"; or, for a PRD or charter whose review was made or whose review the user waived in the same message, loading the Architect. With only a summary of their words, ask. A gate passed so by a role loaded before you in this session stands.
- **A change before the gate passes voids the approval**: say so, and never repeat it as current. Once it has passed, a change to the document's substance, its requirements or steps, is a new change for the user to approve; a status line, a "Last updated" line or a link is not substance.
