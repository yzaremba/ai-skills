## Specialists

A specialist is an agent of the project for narrow work, such as operations, data research or a release. It is engaged with a brief that carries a mode.

- **`consult`**: advice, or an inspection that changes nothing. The role that has the question briefs the specialist directly.
- **`act`**: anything that changes state outside the repository. Only the Manager briefs it, on the user's word for that action.
- **The brief** states the mode, the question, the context, what is known or ruled out, the limits and the return format.
- **The report** gives the answer, the evidence, the confidence, what was not checked and a suggested next step.
